6 · Read this before you point an agent at anything that matters

  • The Lethal Trifecta · Simon WillisonA must read
  • simonwillison.netWorth following generally

Access to private data, exposure to untrusted content, and the ability to communicate outward. Any two are usually fine. All three together, and a document you did not write can instruct your agent to send your data somewhere you did not choose. Everything in this repository was built by an agent holding all three — which is exactly why it ran in a scratch directory with no patient data anywhere near it.

8 · A system prompt worth stealing

The standing instruction I give a model before anything else. The lines that earn their place are the ones giving the model permission to say no: tell me if the request is illogical, wrong, or there is no data after tool calls, and if there is no answer or the resource is not available, say so, do not invent plausible answers.

A model with no licence to fail will produce something rather than nothing, and something rather than nothing is how a fabricated citation ends up in your manuscript.

Read the system prompt Copy it, then cut whatever does not match how you work

9 · The demonstration

An agent answering a real clinical question — fresh versus elective frozen embryo transfer — under a method built so a clinician can check it rather than trust it. A plan committed before any searching. A named primary source behind every number. Two frontier models from two different vendors retrieving those sources separately and instructed to refute rather than agree. An arithmetic rule, applied in code, deciding what survives.

The two models did not agree on everything. They returned different verdicts on 13 of 57 claims. Every disagreement is quoted in full rather than resolved silently, because the disagreements are the part that carries information. Nothing was staged: no disagreement was manufactured and no convergence suppressed. Four things went wrong during the run, including two figures abandoned as AI images after both judges rejected them; all four are on the record in the repository rather than tidied away.
